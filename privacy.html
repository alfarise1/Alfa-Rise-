Privacy Policy

Alfa Rise · Last updated: 19 November 2026

Alfa Rise builds websites, AI chatbots, automation and branding for small businesses. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • When you send an inquiry through our contact form: your name, phone number, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • When you email, message us on WhatsApp or contact us on social media: whatever details you choose to send.
  • If you create an account on our site: your email address and login details, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase).

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and provide a quote or free website check.
  • To deliver and support the work you hire us for.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for anything unrelated to your request.

Where it is stored

Inquiries are stored in our Supabase database. Access is limited to the Alfa Rise team. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the video on our home page is provided by YouTube, which may set its own cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details only as long as needed to respond and to work with you. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email alfarisestore@gmail.com.
