FAQ

Quick answers to common questions

How much does a website cost?

Basic starts at Rs 15,000 / $60, Standard is Rs 35,000 / $140 and Premium is Rs 80,000 / $320. See the Pricing section for what each includes.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the project. We confirm the timeline with you before we start.

Are domain and hosting included?

No, domain and hosting are billed separately. Tell us what you need and we will explain the options.

How many revisions do I get?

Basic includes 2 revisions, Standard includes 4 rounds of revisions.

What is the free website check?

Send us your website or page and we will review it and tell you what can be improved, at no cost.

Do the chatbot and automation have monthly fees?

Yes, a small maintenance fee of Rs 5,000 / $20 per month covers hosting and updates.
